codechef

CodeChef is a competitive programming platform that provides a space for programmers to practice, compete, and improve their coding skills. It was founded in 2009 by Bhavin Turakhia and is now a part of Unacademy

Company History :-- CodeChef was founded in 2009 by Bhavin Turakhia, a serial entrepreneur and billionaire known for creating successful tech companies like Directi, Zeta, and Flock. Initially, CodeChef was launched as a community platform under Directi to promote competitive programming among developers. Over the years, it has grown into one of the leading platforms for coders worldwide.

Practice Problems: A vast collection of problems ranging from beginner to advanced levels across various topics like algorithms, data structures, and math. Contests: Long Challenges: 10-day contests to solve problems at a relaxed pace. Cook-Offs: 2.5-hour contests for more intense problem-solving. Lunchtime: 3-hour contests specifically aimed at school students but open to all.

CodeChef for Schools: They aim to introduce competitive programming to students and run initiatives like "Go for Gold" to encourage participation in international contests like IOI (International Olympiad in Informatics).
